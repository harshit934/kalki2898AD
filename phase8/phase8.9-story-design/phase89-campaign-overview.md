## Continuous campaign spine

Bhairava's paid local work builds ambition for the Complex. Restricted access and the pursued woman connect that ambition to Ashwatthama's protection. Defeat, a bargain and a hidden approach culminate in Shambhala's betrayal and the Mission50 awakening/Manas climax. The game expansion then follows regret, personal visions and disciplined mastery; a proposed later-era transition introduces adult Kalki. Kalki and Bhairava build routes, protect support and defeat the Horizon Keeper at70. Later approach missions culminate in Kalki's direct victory over Supreme Yaskin at100. Every mission record names a consequence and a concrete next connection.

## Exact evidence labels

- MOVIE-CONFIRMED: an event directly verified from supplied footage, scoped to the cited observation.
- DIRECTLY-OBSERVED: what is visibly present in an inspected frame/segment.
- SUBTITLE-SUPPORTED: subtitle evidence without the required visual confirmation.
- USER-APPROVED GAME ADAPTATION: explicit user decision, not a movie-canon claim.
- ORIGINAL GAME CONTENT: proposed game mission arrangement or new expansion.
- INFERRED: interpretation beyond direct evidence.
- UNVERIFIED: insufficient evidence or missing runtime/asset validation.
- CONTRADICTION: incompatible records; resolve explicitly, not by deleting history.
