Phase 5.3 Mission 1 assets

The riverfront district, Bujji field scout, relay, salvage cell, barrier,
precinct wall and Warden geometry were authored for this project with the
Blender generator scripts retained in phase5/phase53-evidence/.
No movie mesh, footage, soundtrack or dialogue recording was used to create
these geometry files. The ripple and costume relief textures are procedural.

The scene reuses the project's existing Poly Haven old_sandstone_02 surfaces
and venice_sunset environment (CC0). See the existing environment credits for
their source records. Draco decoding uses the project's existing decoder and
its retained license. This notice does not clear rights to pre-existing
character assets, movie-related names or legacy media elsewhere in the game.

Private film-reference stills/subtitles under phase5/phase53-evidence/reference-only
are study material only, outside public assets, and must not be distributed.
