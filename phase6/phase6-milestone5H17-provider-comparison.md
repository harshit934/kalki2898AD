## 1. ElevenLabs — strongest candidate for a future isolated audition

### Telugu, male identity and expression

The current **Models** page explicitly lists Telugu te for **eleven_v4** and **eleven_v3**, separately from older multilingual_v2/flash2.5 lists that do not include Telugu. Do not silently use an older cheaper model based on generic Indian-language claims. V4 is now the current expressive family; unlike earlier research, we have checked its current language list. Exact model/voice access and non-Beta status still require confirmation. [Official model/language lists](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models).

The official stock catalogue includes male Adam. That is male-stock availability, not proof that a specific Adam variant is native Telugu, deep, mature or best for Bhairava. Select an eligible original stock voice by documented identity, not an uploaded reference or actor clone; inspect its exact current ID/usage terms later. Prompting docs support voice-dependent audio tags/punctuation and acting direction. These are promising for calm/urgent/emotional categories, not guaranteed results for every Telugu line. [Stock catalogue](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/voice-library/), [current prompting guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/best-practices).

### Rights and cost

Official help permits commercial use for paid non-Beta output subject to rights/policies; free generations and Beta services do not qualify. Selected voice, exact model entitlement, output conditions and agreement-based attribution must still be recorded; paid marketing alone is not approval of every voice. [Commercial policy](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform).

Current monthly Starter lists **USD6/30k credits**; annual equivalentUSD5 is not monthly pricing. Free10k credits are noncommercial. Do not rely on first-month banners or an expired September promotion for a November quote. Exact model/voice credit rate, subscription commitment, currency conversion and account tax total are unverified; do not equate credits to characters without the exact SKU. [Pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing).

Terms cover recurring subscriptions, optional recurring credit purchases, overages and applicable taxes. No verified project-specific India GST invoice/rate established here; quoted price is not guaranteed landed cost. Do not enable overage/recharge or buy a plan. [Terms, payment/credit provisions](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use).

### Billing controls, API and Windows

Official API-key docs specify scope restrictions, **custom credit quota** and optional expiry/IP restrictions. This is concrete vendor-side usage-control documentation, stronger than a purely local estimate. It is **not proof of exact INR10 all-in enforcement**, a specific account's configured quota, atomic in-flight cutoff or total subscription/tax cap. Quotas restrict use, not already incurred subscription charges. New subscriptions use Pay As You Go rather than assuming legacy usage-billing toggles; inspect the actual account's configuration later. [API-key controls](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/workspaces/api-keys), [billing distinctions](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/administration/billing).

HTTPS authoring API is usable from Windows without local neural-model RAM/VRAM. No SDK/package required by this research. Store a future credential only in an isolated backend/local authoring process, never Vite/client assets. Export approved recordings for offline playback; do not use live cloud calls on the combat frame path. Actual response latency/WAV format/phone playback remain untested.

## 2. Google Cloud — explicit Telugu male catalogue and transparent character pricing

### Telugu and delivery

Official catalogue explicitly labels Telugu te-IN male **Achird/Charon** in Chirp3 HD. These names are candidates, not voice-quality judgments. Current Chirp3 docs support a subset of SSML for synchronous synthesis (not streaming); prosody/pause capability does not establish a controlled Telugu emotional-acting style. Check exact tags/method/locale rather than obsolete claims that Chirp3 has no SSML at all. [Voice catalogue](https://docs.cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/docs/list-voices-and-types), [Chirp3 controls](https://docs.cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/docs/chirp3-hd).

### Rights, pricing and GST

Official basics permits using created audio files in applications/media subject to Cloud terms. Treat that as a commercial application route, not a promise of exclusive output copyright, underlying dataset rights or every stock-voice exception. No account contract inspected. [Audio use and formats](https://docs.cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/docs/basics), [Cloud terms](https://cloud.google.com/terms).

Pricing lists Chirp3 HD first1M characters free and **USD30/1M thereafter**; billing must be enabled and excess usage is automatically charged. A free allowance is not a spend cap. Account remaining free usage/currency/tier unknown. [Official pricing](https://cloud.google.com/text-to-speech/pricing).

Official tax documentation distinguishes India seller/contract: Google Cloud India sales to India billing addresses attract18% GST regardless of GSTIN; Google Asia Pacific contracts with India customers have stated0%/18% treatment depending on valid GSTIN. This does not establish which applies here; invoice/seller and tax information must be verified before a final quote. No universal tax-free assumption. [Official India tax section](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/resources/vat-overview#india).

### Cap caveat and technical fit

Current official spend-cap docs list Gemini API, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, Cloud Run and functions—not Cloud Text-to-Speech. Enforcement is based on estimated cost and is not instant; in-flight calls complete and overages can be billed. Do not assume a generic budget or a cap on another service safely caps Chirp3 TTS, or assert all Google budgets lack enforcement anywhere. Quota reductions are throughput controls, not automatically a monthly monetary maximum. No Google TTS INR10 hard guarantee demonstrated. [Eligible services and limitations](https://docs.cloud.google.com/billing/docs/how-to/budgets-spend-caps).

Windows REST/IAM authoring and base64/LINEAR16 export are documented; actual authentication/format/performance untested. More billing/IAM setup than a simple fixed-credit audition; no resources created. Offline playback remains the proposed integration, not a live request per combat line.
