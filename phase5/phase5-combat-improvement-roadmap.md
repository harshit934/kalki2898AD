# Combat improvement roadmap — proposal only

15 November 2026. No implementation authorized or started. Preserve Phase5 CONDITIONAL ACCEPT, unlimited sprint, default environment and opt-in Kashi separation. Improve one existing playable combat slice before any campaign expansion; do not move later campaign requirements into Phase5.

Effort is estimated hands-on engineering/art/QA time, not an elapsed-time promise. Ranges assume existing working systems, supplied/licensed assets when needed and approval availability; bespoke art can exceed them. Seven packages total **260–500 hours**, about **7–13 full-time person-weeks** at40h/week before approval/licensing delays. Parallel artists can shorten calendar time, not total work. A first single-enemy/single-arena review slice can precede the full set. These estimates do not cover ten wholly bespoke cinematic-quality boss models or a full city rebuild; scope those individually after the slice.

1. **Core player/enemy visual quality — essential; 48–88h.** Keep protected player mesh unchanged. Establish fixed-camera material/skin/cloth/metal validation, improve approved adapters and one enemy silhouette with legitimate asset provenance. Test deformation, weapons, costume, all shadow angles and target-distance readability. Gate: identical playable camera comparisons visibly improve detail without silhouette loss; protected hashes unchanged. Dependency: asset rights and approval for any protected modification. Reuse soldier rig only where deformation/identity supports the role; replacement is conditional, not automatic.
2. **Camera and movement/attack animation — essential; 40–72h.** Audit moving combat recordings first; retain clearance/smoothing. Add approved non-destructive animation coverage for light/heavy/recovery/dodge/hit-react where absent. Gate: obstacle/arena transitions, strafing, simultaneous foes and portrait/landscape preserve visible player/opponent; no snaps, clipping or sliding in reviewed recordings. Do not claim camera is currently broken from a still.
3. **Combat responsiveness and contact feedback — essential; 32–64h.** Measure input→animation→damage→sound sequence, walls/ranges/moving targets and interrupted attacks. Decide whether a small timing adjustment is justified before changing the immediate range/LOS contract. Add restrained readable hit/block cues with rights-cleared sound. Gate: declared contact windows match authored animation, no duplicate/through-wall damage, dodge/block and mobile release cleanup remain correct. Do not introduce stamina or redesign rewards.
4. **Bosses and health presentation — essential; 40–80h.** Preserve all ten profiles and defeat/reward contracts. Polish one boss model/attack telegraph/defeat slice and reuse proven presentation components, not identical boss behavior. Keep named numeric boss/player bars; test long names, health changes, all phases and portrait action visibility. Gate: every existing boss functional regression passes; art review remains separate. Ten fully bespoke production assets require a separately estimated expansion of this package.
5. **Arena, lighting and effects — essential for target quality; 64–120h.** Finish one connected arena/approach visible from normal gameplay cameras: foreground dressing, varied believable surfaces, spatial depth and shadow/readability balance. Budget effects and draw calls before increasing density. Gate: in-game same-camera comparison against supplied reference qualities; no concept-render acceptance; Kashi stays opt-in until independent approval.
6. **HUD polish — targeted; 12–24h.** Preserve Phase5 UI behavior, bars and three modes. Reduce portrait information footprint, verify enemy bar legibility and central gameplay safety. Gate: desktop1920×1080/1280×720, portrait430×932 and landscape932×430; no clipped labels, accidental page scroll or touch interception; current assistant overlays included. Unlimited sprint remains unchanged. Major menu redesign is optional and out of this correction scope.
7. **Performance verification/optimization — essential gate; 24–52h.** Measure during each package; reserve this allowance for a final bounded profile and verified bottleneck repairs. Same build, power condition, buffer and scenario; three comparable active-fight runs of at least60s per representative desktop/emulated-mobile case, plus physical device when available. Record median/p95/p99/max, all >100ms gaps and loading separately. Target1080p ~30FPS (33.3ms budget); proposed practical gate median≤35ms,p95≤40ms,p99≤50ms for matched desktop fights, with recurring visible >100ms interruptions investigated and remaining isolated transition gaps explicitly approved. These are proposed criteria, not demonstrated current results or perfect-FPS requirements. No resolution reduction to manufacture improvement. Physical-device acceptance requires a named device/profile and separate budget.

## Execution safeguards and dependencies

Before approved edits: snapshot affected files and manifests; retain original route and query-gated variant. No save schema/reward/unlock changes. Use disposable profiles only. Test affected gameplay after each package, build/lint before review, verify47 protected files and preserved campaign copies. Restore only the changed source/adapter from its own backup on regression; never reset campaign storage or overwrite rollback assets. Keep audio rights blocking and packaging exclusions.

Optional later work: broad crowd simulation, full-city expansion, expensive volumetrics/reflections, elaborate camera shake, additional menu redesign and ten bespoke cinematic defeat productions. They must not displace core asset, animation, contact and stability work. No engine migration is justified by this audit.

Before campaign expansion: approve the representative combat slice, demonstrate animation/contact clarity and stable repeated active fights, resolve or explicitly accept portrait hitches, retain all existing boss/control/save regressions. Functional health bars and passing builds alone are insufficient.
